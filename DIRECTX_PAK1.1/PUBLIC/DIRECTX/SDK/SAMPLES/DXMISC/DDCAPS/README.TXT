DirectDraw Caps Viewer
----------------------

This utility allows you to examine the DirectDraw capabilities of a device.
The main window displays all supported screen modes and resolutions.

Choose from the Driver and HEL menus to see a list of capabilities for
the following items:

    Driver
        Driver Caps
        Driver Color Key Caps
        Driver FX Caps
        Driver Palette Caps
        Driver Surface Caps

    HEL
        HEL Caps
        HEL Color Key Caps
        HEL FX Caps
        HEL Palette Caps
        HEL Surface Caps
